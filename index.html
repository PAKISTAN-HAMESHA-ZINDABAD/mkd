Process & Nutrition

Simple farm methods that preserve real protein and minerals

When dairy is made without fillers or shortcuts, every part of the milk works together—rich strained yogurt for the bowl, mineral-rich whey water for recovery, and cultured butterfat for the kitchen.

01. Approx. 10g Protein / 100g

Slow-Strained Greek Yogurt

Instead of adding milk powder, cornstarch, or commercial thickeners, we culture pure cow milk and let it drain gently through muslin cloth for 12 hours. Nearly three litres of milk go into one kilogram of dense, creamy yogurt.

02. Zero Added Sugar · Natural Electrolytes

Natural Whey Water Recovery

The pale liquid that separates during Greek yogurt straining is pure sweet-acid whey—naturally holding potassium, calcium, and mild sodium. Chilled and bottled fresh, it offers light hydration after workouts or warm Lahore afternoons.

03. Cultured Curd Churn · Unrefined Pressing

Traditional Bilona Desi Ghee & Cold-Pressed Oil

Our desi ghee begins as whole set dahi churned with a wooden bilona to separate white butter before slow simmering. For daily cooking, our canola oil is cold-pressed mechanically below 45°C without hexane solvents.
